Many Stories, One Destination

Located in Hyderabad, ASR CONVENTION & RESORT was envisioned to redefine large-scale event hosting. We set out to create a sanctuary where monumental gatherings feel intimate, seamless, and dignified.

With our 25,000 sq. ft. pillar-less convention arena, lush resort lawns, and swimming pool, ASR seamlessly accommodates every scale — from weddings of 5,000 guests to private retreats and corporate summits.
