Common Inquiries

Our main pillar-less hall accommodates up to 3,000 guests in formal theater/wedding seating and up to 5,000 for floating receptions. Our outdoor lawns and poolside terrace accommodate 200 to 1,500 guests for smaller gatherings or pre-wedding functions.

Yes. While we can recommend reputable catering partners and decor specialists across Hyderabad, clients are also welcome to bring their own catering teams and decorators with prior written coordination with our venue office.

We have a paved on-site parking zone that accommodates over 500+ cars and 800+ two-wheelers. Valet drivers and traffic staff ensure smooth entry and exit during large events.

Yes. We offer air-conditioned bridal dressing suites attached to the main hall, as well as deluxe rooms in our resort wing for overnight family stays and weekend functions.

The venue is equipped with 100% heavy-duty acoustic power generators with automatic transfer switches, ensuring zero power interruption. Centralized air-conditioning keeps the hall comfortable across all seasons.

Bookings are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis upon receipt of a 50% advance deposit. The remaining balance is payable prior to the start of the event.
